## Introduction

Building libraries is hard, but picking the right bundler for your library is significantly harder.

There are so many tools out there, e.g

- rollup
- rolldown
- webpack
- vite
- tsup
- esbuild
- unbuild
- tsdown

...and many other more not mentioned that you might not have heard of

## Picking the right bundler

There are a few things to take note of before picking a bundler.

1. What are my targets? _(e.g Is it for just Vue, just Nuxt, or both?)_
2. Do I plan to support both `browser` and `node` runtimes?
3. What format will the users will be utilizing? _(e.g `esm`, `cjs` or `umd`)_
4. How big will my library be?

### Understanding core bundlers

Libraries like `rollup`, `rolldown` and `webpack` are the core and barebone
engines that power other bundlers that are usually used today

e.g

| Core Bundler | Child Bundlers |
|--------------|----------------|
| `rollup` | `tsup`, `vite` |
| `rolldown` | `tsdown` |
| `esbuild` | `vite` |

> Vite appears twice because it uses esbuild in dev, and rollup for production builds

When building libraries, I would highly suggest, you pick one of the child bundlers e.g
`vite`, `tsdown`, `tsup`, ...etc if your library is not as big and complex,
as they will work easy for you.

### Decision table

The table below can guide your picking

| Question | Yes! | No. |
|----------|------|----|
| Am I building a small, simple library with very little dependencies | `tsdown` | `rollup` |
| Will my library be working as a plugin? | `rollup` | `tsdown` |
| Is it a __Nuxt__ only module? | `nuxt-module-builder` (unbuild) | `tsdown` |
| Is it a __Vue__ only module? | `tsdown` | -- |
| Is my project complex requiring transformations of certain file types? | `rollup` | `tsdown` |
| Can I experiment with bleeding edge technology? | `rolldown` (tsdown) | `rollup` (tsup) |
